Get started

One package.
Your tests as they are.

Add the package, point it at your project, sign your machine in, and run your tests the way you always do. Shield heals and RunSights reports from the first run.

You need a TAMASH project. No trial yet? Request one ↗, or book a demo.

  1. 01

    Add the package and point it at the project

    Copy the project ID (prj_…) from Administration, Projects in the portal. It is not a secret.

    init adds two lines to playwright.config: one for Shield, one for the RunSights reporter. It sets an actionTimeout when there is none and writes the project ID to tamash.config.json. It prints every change; --dry-run only shows them. Commit both files. Your specs keep importing from @playwright/test.

  2. 02

    Sign in on your machine

    The portal opens in your browser and you approve the machine. Your email and a personal access token are saved in your home folder, never in the repository, and your runs show under your name.

    On a machine with no browser, make a token under My profile, Access tokens and use npx tamash-runsights login --token <token> --email <your email>.

  3. 03

    Give your pipeline a service token

    In the portal, open the project's CI connection and create a service token. Store it in your CI system as the secret TAMASH_ACCESS_TOKEN. Nothing else is needed: the project comes from tamash.config.json.

    The help centre has the same for Azure DevOps, GitLab and Jenkins ↗.

  4. 04

    Run as usual

    Nothing in your command line changes. A healed locator is one line in the console, and the run ends with its number in RunSights.

    Cucumber.js (8 and later): two lines in the profile instead, before your own support code. Hooks and step definitions do not change.

  5. 05

    Check the setup and open the portal

    npx tamash-runsights doctor checks the setup. When TAMASH cannot work for a run, your tests still run exactly as they do without it, and the console says once why.

    RunSights

    The run you just sent, with every test, where it ran and who ran it. Each failure says why it happened.

    Shield

    The heals of the run, by test: the locator that broke, the one that worked, and anything that needs a look.

    Make heals permanent

    apply-heals writes them into your code and verify-heals proves them with healing off.

The settings you may need.
