The idea

Two stages of quality.
Never mixed.

What is built while the feature is made, and what is proven when the release is tested. Forge keeps them apart, and a person promotes from one to the other.

Development stage

Built while the feature is made

Scenarios, automated specs, pages and locators, findings and proposals from the GenEx Agent, developers and QA. Informal, continuous, never counted as release results.

  • Explore: what the agent found in the application
  • Proposals waiting for a person's decision
  • Development quality dashboard
promote→
Release stage

Proven when the release is tested

Cycles run against accepted test versions, with evidence on every attempt and sign-off at the end. The formal record a release is judged on.

  • Published test cases, versioned
  • Cycles, executions, defects and evidence
  • Release health, readiness and sign-off
Five modules

Everything a release needs,
in the order a team meets it.

01

Test cases

A versioned library in folders: steps tables or Gherkin, custom fields, import and export. Authors publish directly or another author approves.

02

Test cycles

Chosen tests in an environment over a period: plan, assign, run, then readiness and sign-off.

03

Executions

A step-by-step runner on desktop or phone. Every attempt keeps its evidence and defects, and CI results join the same history.

04

Reports

Ready-made reports, a builder, traceability to requirements, exports and fixed snapshots.

05

Dashboard

Where the project stands and what needs attention, arranged by each person.

The GenEx Agent inside Forge

It explores, drafts and validates.
People decide.

  • Explore. The agent maps screens, fields, controls and server calls, and reports findings as broken, should fix, or a note.
  • Propose. From a finding or a scenario it drafts a test case. An author accepts, edits or declines it.
  • Generate. Reusable Playwright or Cucumber automation, with pages and locators the team can see.
  • Validate. It runs scenarios against the developed feature. Its runs stay in the development stage until a person promotes them.

The agent never presses Save, Delete or Submit.

AI that suggests. People who decide.

Less writing. Less planning.
The same judgement.

Every suggestion is something to accept, edit or decline. None changes a result.
