In your workflow

Locally and in CI, the same way.

On a laptop

Sign the machine in once with npx tamash-shield login. From then on every run heals as it goes and reaches RunSights in your name.

In CI

The project key and a service token as secrets. Healing runs the same way, and apply-heals after the job opens the pull request.

What Shield never touches

  • expect() assertions. A silently "fixed" assertion could hide a real bug.
  • Drag and drop, and waitFor. One side of a drag cannot be guessed from the other, and a wait that ran out may be a correct outcome.

What is sent to the service

  • When a locator breaks: the action, the element's description, the error, and the page's accessibility outline with secrets removed.
  • Never the values your tests type or your test data. Nothing of the page is stored.
Works with

Your runner, your tests, as they are.

Playwright Test

Import test and expect from @vibetestq/tamash-shield instead of @playwright/test; init does it for you. Everything else stays.

Playwright library and page objects

Wrap a page with bindPageActions where you create it. Chain .describe('Pay now button') on plain CSS locators so the healer knows what it is looking for.

Cucumber.js

Steps that drive Playwright heal the same way, and the RunSights formatter records each scenario and the Playwright calls it made.
