## AI Era Indicator
**AI Quality Architecture Role** — Designs enterprise AI quality systems, owns responsible AI testing governance, and architects AI SDLC quality frameworks.

## Description
We are seeking an experienced Test Architect who can lead quality engineering into the AI era. The ideal candidate will design enterprise-grade AI quality architectures, build LLM evaluation infrastructures, establish responsible AI testing governance, and define AI SDLC quality frameworks. This role requires both deep traditional test architecture expertise and cutting-edge AI/ML quality engineering leadership.

## Responsibilities
- Design enterprise AI quality architecture: LLM evaluation platforms, MLOps quality systems, responsible AI testing infrastructure
- Define AI SDLC quality frameworks: quality gates at every phase from AI-assisted requirements to AI model production monitoring
- Lead AI testing strategy across the organization: AI-augmented QA practices, LLM product quality, agentic AI testing
- Architect scalable GenAI test generation pipelines converting enterprise requirements into automated test suites
- Design responsible AI testing programs: bias testing infrastructure, safety evaluation pipelines, fairness validation systems
- Build AI compliance quality architecture for EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, and ISO/IEC 42001
- Evaluate and recommend AI testing tools, platforms, and vendor solutions for enterprise adoption
- Design end-to-end test architectures for enterprise AI applications including RAG systems, AI agents, and ML platforms
- Lead development of AI-native testing frameworks and evaluation harnesses
- Provide technical leadership and AI testing mentorship to test leads and SDET teams
- Establish AI quality engineering standards, best practices, and competency frameworks
- Oversee performance, security (OWASP LLM Top 10), and adversarial testing for AI systems
- Participate in AI governance boards and provide quality engineering perspective
- Contribute to innovation in AI testing: publish standards, present at conferences, drive industry practices
- Lead proof-of-concept projects for emerging AI testing technologies and methodologies

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Engineering, or related field
- 10-15 years of experience in software testing and quality assurance, with 3+ years in AI quality leadership
- Proven experience as a Test Architect designing enterprise-scale quality systems
- Strong expertise in AI/LLM testing, MLOps quality, and responsible AI evaluation
- Deep knowledge of LLM evaluation frameworks: RAGAS, DeepEval, LangSmith, Promptfoo
- Understanding of AI/ML architecture: transformers, RAG, fine-tuning, AI agents, MLOps
- Experience with AI governance frameworks: EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001
- Familiarity with AI safety testing: OWASP LLM Top 10, red teaming, adversarial testing
- Experience with cloud AI platforms (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI, Azure AI)
- Strong expertise in traditional test automation (Selenium, Playwright, REST testing)
- Proficiency in Python and/or Java for test framework design
- Excellent leadership, communication, executive presentation, and stakeholder management skills
- ISTQB AI Testing, Advanced Test Manager, or equivalent certification preferred
