## Job Description
We are seeking a motivated Junior Manual Tester to join our quality assurance team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for ensuring the quality and reliability of software applications through manual testing processes. This role involves creating test cases, executing tests, and reporting defects to contribute to the delivery of high-quality software products.

## Responsibilities
- Create, execute, and maintain manual test cases and test scripts using AI assistance (ChatGPT, Claude)
- Use AI co-pilots to generate test cases from user stories, requirements, and acceptance criteria
- Perform functional, regression, sanity, and user acceptance testing
- Test AI-powered features (chatbots, recommendation engines, search suggestions) for expected behavior
- Identify, document, and track software defects using AI-enhanced defect reporting templates
- Collaborate with development teams to understand requirements and provide feedback
- Participate in agile development processes and sprint planning
- Analyze test results and provide detailed reports on software quality
- Use AI-powered exploratory testing heuristics to identify edge cases
- Ensure compliance with testing standards and best practices
- Assist in the preparation of test plans and test strategies
- Learn to evaluate simple AI/LLM feature outputs for accuracy and expected responses

## Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or related field
- 0-2 years of experience in manual testing or software quality assurance
- Basic understanding of software development life cycle (SDLC) and testing methodologies
- Familiarity with testing tools like JIRA, Selenium (basic), and SQL
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Good communication and teamwork abilities
- Knowledge of agile methodologies is a plus
