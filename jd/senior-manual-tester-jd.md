## Description
We are seeking a Senior Manual Tester to join our QA team and ensure the quality and functionality of our software applications through comprehensive manual testing. The ideal candidate will have extensive experience in manual testing methodologies, with a focus on functional, regression, and exploratory testing. You will work closely with development teams to identify defects, validate requirements, and ensure high-quality software delivery.

## Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and execute detailed manual test cases and test scenarios for web, mobile, and API applications
- Use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) to generate comprehensive test suites from requirements, PRDs, and user stories
- Perform functional, regression, integration, and exploratory testing to identify defects and ensure software quality
- Lead quality validation of AI-powered features: test LLM chatbot responses, AI recommendations, and GenAI-assisted workflows
- Evaluate AI feature outputs for hallucination, relevance, safety, consistency, and factual accuracy
- Perform adversarial prompt testing for AI features to identify edge cases and safety risks
- Collaborate with business analysts and developers to understand requirements and create comprehensive test plans
- Execute manual tests, document results, and report defects using AI-enhanced defect templates
- Conduct API testing validating both traditional and AI-powered endpoints
- Perform database testing to ensure data integrity and accuracy
- Participate in Agile/Scrum ceremonies and provide feedback on user stories and acceptance criteria
- Create and maintain AI-generated test data for comprehensive coverage scenarios
- Mentor junior testers on AI tool adoption and AI feature testing approaches
- Ensure compliance with testing standards and best practices
- Contribute to responsible AI testing practices: document bias incidents, content safety issues, and AI quality metrics
