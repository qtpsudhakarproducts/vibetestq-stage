## Job Description
We are seeking a Lead AI SDLC Quality Engineer who will redesign the entire Software Development Lifecycle quality framework for an AI-first engineering organization. This role goes beyond traditional QA to embed quality thinking at every phase of how AI systems are designed, built, trained, deployed, and monitored. You will be the owner of quality in the AI SDLC — ensuring that every transition from one phase to the next is gated by validated quality criteria.

## Responsibilities
- Design the comprehensive Quality Engineering framework for the AI SDLC: quality gates, acceptance criteria, and review processes at every phase
- Drive AI requirements quality: establish AI-assisted requirement review processes, validate requirement testability, and define AI feature acceptance criteria
- Own AI code quality standards: develop policies for AI-generated code (Copilot, Cursor), define review checklists, and build automated code quality analysis
- Design data quality gates for ML workflows: validate training data quality, monitor data drift, and enforce schema/distribution standards
- Establish model quality gates: define model evaluation criteria, automate benchmark comparisons, and gatekeep model promotion through stages
- Build MLOps quality integration: embed quality checks in CI/CD for ML training, evaluation, and deployment pipelines
- Define AI deployment quality standards: canary testing policies, A/B test quality criteria, shadow deployment validation, and rollback triggers
- Lead AI production quality monitoring: define observability standards, quality SLAs for AI in production, and alerting thresholds
- Create the AI Quality Policy Framework: document standards, review templates, quality checklists, and compliance evidence requirements
- Champion developer experience for quality: build internal AI quality portals, shared test infrastructure, and self-service quality tooling
- Drive AI security quality in SDLC: threat modeling for AI features, SAST/DAST for AI-generated code, ML supply chain security
- Collaborate with platform engineering to build shared quality infrastructure: test environments for LLMs, GPU test sandboxes, inference validation environments
- Lead regulatory compliance quality for AI SDLC: build evidence collection, audit trails, and risk management documentation for EU AI Act
- Partner with EPD (Engineering, Product, Design) leadership to evolve quality-as-culture across AI development teams
- Build and mentor a team of quality engineers embedded in AI product development workflows
