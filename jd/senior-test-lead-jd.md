## Description## Description
We are seeking an experienced Senior Test Lead to oversee testing activities and lead a team of QA professionals. The ideal candidate will have extensive experience in test management, automation frameworks, and leading cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality software products. This role involves strategic planning, team leadership, and ensuring comprehensive test coverage across various domains and technologies.

## Responsibilities
- Lead AI-era QA teams with focus on GitHub Copilot adoption, AI test generation, and self-healing automation implementation
- Define and implement AI-first test strategies using LLMs for test case generation and intelligent CI/CD quality gates
- Develop comprehensive test strategies and plans for both traditional software and AI-powered product features
- Own quality validation for AI features: validate LLM outputs, AI chatbot responses, and AI-powered workflow correctness
- Oversee design and execution of manual and AI-augmented automated test cases
- Implement AI-powered CI/CD quality gates: intelligent test selection, ML flake detection, anomaly alerting
- Monitor test execution with AI analytics, analyze results, and report quality metrics and trends
- Manage defect tracking including AI-specific defects: hallucination reports, model drift, AI safety issues
- Ensure compliance with testing standards, AI governance policies, and industry best practices
- Apply responsible AI testing: validate AI features for bias, content safety, and data privacy risks
- Lead performance, security (including OWASP LLM Top 10 awareness), and usability testing
- Foster a culture of AI-augmented quality and continuous improvement within the team
- Coordinate with stakeholders on AI quality metrics, testing progress, and AI risk communication
- Mentor junior and mid-level QA engineers on AI tool adoption and AI testing methodologies
- Evaluate and recommend emerging AI testing tools and platforms for team adoption

- Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills
- Knowledge of CI/CD practices and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure)
- ISTQB or equivalent certification preferred
- Experience in embedded testing, ETL, or domain-specific testing is a plus
