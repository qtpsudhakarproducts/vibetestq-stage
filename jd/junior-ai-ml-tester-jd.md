## Job Description
We are seeking a Junior AI/ML Systems Tester to join our Quality Engineering team and contribute to validating AI-powered applications. This is a specialized entry-level role for individuals passionate about understanding how AI systems behave, fail, and improve. You will test LLM responses, AI feature outputs, and ML model predictions to ensure they are accurate, fair, and reliable.

## Responsibilities
- Design and execute test cases for AI/ML features including chatbots, recommendation engines, and generative AI outputs
- Evaluate LLM-generated responses for quality, relevance, factual accuracy, and hallucination risks
- Perform adversarial prompt testing to identify model vulnerabilities (jailbreaks, prompt injections)
- Test AI model APIs for correctness, latency, and edge case handling
- Validate model outputs against expected benchmarks and acceptance criteria
- Document AI-specific defects including model drift, bias indicators, and hallucination patterns
- Support data validation testing for training datasets and inference pipelines
- Collaborate with ML engineers and product teams to define AI testing acceptance criteria
- Learn and apply AI evaluation frameworks (RAGAS, DeepEval, LangSmith)
- Monitor basic model performance metrics and flag regressions in AI behavior
- Write test reports covering AI quality dimensions: Accuracy, Consistency, Safety, Fairness
