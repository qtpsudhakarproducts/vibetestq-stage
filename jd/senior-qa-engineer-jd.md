## Description
We are seeking an experienced Senior QA Engineer to join our team and play a pivotal role in ensuring the quality and reliability of our software products. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in both manual and automated testing, with expertise in modern testing tools and frameworks. You will work closely with development teams to identify defects early, implement robust testing strategies, and contribute to continuous improvement of our QA processes.

## Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and execute comprehensive test plans for web, mobile, and API applications including AI-powered features
- Use AI tools (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude) to generate, enhance, and maintain automated test scripts
- Implement self-healing automation (Testim, Mabl, Healenium) to reduce test maintenance overhead
- Lead quality validation for AI-powered product features: validate LLM chatbot responses, AI recommendations, and GenAI workflows
- Evaluate AI feature outputs for accuracy, hallucination, relevance, and safety dimensions
- Develop and maintain automated test scripts using Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, and REST Assured
- Perform manual testing including functional, regression, performance, and API testing
- Integrate AI-assisted automation into CI/CD pipelines with intelligent quality gates
- Identify, document, and track defects including AI-specific quality issues
- Conduct performance testing using JMeter, Locust, and AI-powered analytics
- Mentor junior QA engineers on AI tool adoption and AI testing best practices
- Apply prompt engineering for test generation, defect analysis, and exploratory testing heuristics
- Participate in code reviews providing feedback on testability and AI-readiness
- Ensure compliance with QA methodologies, AI governance standards, and industry best practices
- Contribute to responsible AI testing: validate AI features for bias indicators, content safety, and data privacy

## Qualifications

- Strong proficiency in automation tools: Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, REST Assured

- Knowledge of API testing, performance testing, and database testing (SQL, DynamoDB)
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies, CI/CD, version control (Git), and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure)

- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
- Strong communication and collaboration abilities
- ISTQB or equivalent certification is a plus
- Experience with AI testing, mobile testing, and e-commerce platforms is advantageous
