## Description
We are seeking an experienced Lead QA Engineer to lead our quality assurance efforts and ensure the delivery of high-quality software products. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in both manual and automated testing, with expertise in leading QA teams and implementing best practices in software testing. This role involves overseeing test strategies, mentoring junior team members, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver robust, scalable solutions.

## Responsibilities
- Lead and manage QA teams, driving AI tool adoption and mentoring engineers on AI-augmented testing practices
- Define and own AI quality engineering strategy: AI-assisted test automation, LLM feature quality, and responsible AI testing
- Design and implement AI-native test automation frameworks using Playwright, Selenium enhanced by GitHub Copilot and LLMs
- Implement self-healing test platforms and AI-powered visual regression systems for large-scale test maintenance
- Own quality validation for AI-powered product features: LLM integrations, AI chatbots, recommendation engines, and GenAI workflows
- Integrate AI quality gates into CI/CD pipelines: intelligent test selection, ML flake detection, anomaly alerting
- Lead responsible AI testing initiatives: validate AI features for bias, safety, content quality, and hallucination risks
- Perform and lead manual testing, including functional, regression, performance, and security testing
- Analyze test results using AI-powered analytics, identify patterns, and drive engineering quality improvements
- Ensure compliance with quality standards, AI governance requirements, and industry best practices
- Participate in requirement analysis and provide input on testability, AI-readiness, and quality aspects
- Monitor and improve testing processes, AI toolchain adoption, and quality metrics
- Conduct root cause analysis for production issues including AI model behavior regressions
- Lead QA efforts across AI-powered projects involving TensorFlow, Databricks, cloud AI services, and ML platforms
- Mentor team members on AI tools, prompt engineering, and AI testing methodologies
