## Job Description
We are seeking a Senior Responsible AI Tester who will own the ethical quality dimensions of AI systems. With global AI regulations accelerating and societal expectations rising, this role ensures that AI products are safe, fair, transparent, privacy-preserving, and aligned with human values before they reach users. You will work at the intersection of technical testing, ethics, policy, and legal compliance.

## Responsibilities
- Design and execute comprehensive responsible AI test plans covering safety, fairness, privacy, transparency, and accountability
- Perform bias testing across AI/ML models using statistical fairness metrics: demographic parity, equalized odds, disparate impact, counterfactual fairness
- Lead red teaming exercises for AI safety: eliciting harmful outputs, testing content filters, verifying alignment and refusal behaviors
- Test AI system explainability: validate that model decisions are interpretable and explainable through SHAP/LIME outputs and model cards
- Evaluate privacy risks in AI systems: test for PII leakage in LLM outputs, membership inference vulnerabilities, and training data extraction attacks
- Conduct adversarial robustness testing: evaluate model resistance to adversarial inputs, data distribution shifts, and model evasion attacks
- Validate AI content safety systems: test moderation classifiers, toxicity filters, harmful content detection pipelines
- Create comprehensive AI audit evidence packages for regulatory compliance (EU AI Act Article 9, NIST AI RMF controls)
- Collaborate with legal, compliance, ethics, and product teams to define responsible AI acceptance criteria
- Define and monitor responsible AI KPIs: bias metrics trends, safety test coverage, fairness incident rates
- Build responsible AI testing checklists, playbooks, and review templates for cross-functional teams
- Lead AI risk assessments and produce risk-tiered testing plans based on AI system criticality
- Represent quality engineering in AI ethics review boards and governance committees
- Stay ahead of emerging regulations and update testing programs for EU AI Act, India AI governance, and international standards

## Responsible AI Testing Taxonomy
| Pillar | Test Types | Standards |
|---|---|---|
| Safety | Harm elicitation, content safety, refusal verification | NIST AI RMF, OWASP LLM |
| Fairness | Demographic bias, disparate impact tests | EU AI Act Article 10 |
| Transparency | Explainability validation, model card accuracy | ISO/IEC 42001 |
| Privacy | PII leakage, membership inference | GDPR + AI Act |
| Accountability | Audit trails, decision logging | EU AI Act Article 14 |
| Robustness | Adversarial attacks, distribution shift | NIST AI RMF MS-2 |
