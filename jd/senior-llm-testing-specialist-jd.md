## Job Description
We are seeking a Senior LLM Testing Specialist who has mastered the science and art of evaluating Large Language Models in production environments. You will lead the design and execution of comprehensive LLM evaluation programs, define quality benchmarks for GenAI products, build scalable evaluation infrastructure, and serve as the organization's expert on AI quality and safety testing.

## Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end LLM evaluation program: define quality dimensions, build evaluation harnesses, and manage benchmark evolution
- Design and execute advanced adversarial testing campaigns: prompt injection, jailbreaking, goal hijacking, indirect prompt injection
- Build scalable automated evaluation pipelines using RAGAS, DeepEval, LangSmith, or custom Python frameworks
- Create and curate golden evaluation datasets — domain-specific question sets, adversarial test sets, and regression benchmarks
- Lead red teaming exercises for GenAI products — structured risk identification and attack surface mapping
- Evaluate AI agent systems: agentic loop quality, tool-calling accuracy, memory consistency, multi-turn coherence
- Design LLM-as-Judge evaluation systems with calibrated rubrics — measure, validate, and improve judge reliability
- Benchmark competing LLM versions, fine-tuned models, and RAG configurations for cost-quality-latency tradeoffs
- Define and monitor production LLM quality SLAs: hallucination rate targets, accuracy floors, latency ceilings
- Integrate LLM evaluation into MLOps CI/CD pipelines for continuous quality regression testing
- Produce detailed evaluation reports for executive and product leadership: quality trends, risk surface, improvement recommendations
- Collaborate with AI researchers, ML engineers, and ethics teams on safety, bias, and fairness evaluation
- Contribute to industry-standard evaluation frameworks and methodology publications
- Advise legal and compliance teams on LLM quality evidence for regulatory requirements (EU AI Act, AI executive orders)

|---|---|---|
| Factual Accuracy | Benchmark datasets, fact-checking | RAGAS, TruthfulQA |
| Hallucination Rate | Grounding verification, citation checks | DeepEval, RAG faithfulness |

| Consistency | Paraphrase invariance, temperature testing | Custom harness |
