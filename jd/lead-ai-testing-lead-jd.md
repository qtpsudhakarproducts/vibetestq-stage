## Job Description
We are seeking a Lead AI Testing Engineer to head our AI quality engineering function and set the standard for how the organization tests AI-powered products and leverages AI in its testing processes. You will lead a team of senior, mid-level, and junior AI QA engineers, drive organizational quality strategy, and hold accountability for AI product quality across the full AI SDLC.

## Responsibilities
- Lead and grow a team of AI quality engineers across all specializations: AI-augmented testers, LLM quality engineers, MLOps QAs, and responsible AI testers
- Define organization-wide AI testing strategy and roadmap — covering AI-augmented QA practices and AI product testing quality programs
- Own AI quality engineering KPIs: defect escape rate, automation coverage, LLM quality benchmarks, safety testing coverage, bias incident rate
- Build and scale AI-native test automation infrastructure that supports multiple product teams simultaneously
- Establish AI testing standards, playbooks, and competency frameworks — publish through wiki, training, and certification paths
- Lead testing strategy for flagship GenAI products: LLM copilots, AI agents, RAG-based assistants, and multimodal AI features
- Drive adoption of AI tools across all QA functions: GitHub Copilot, AI test generation platforms, intelligent CI/CD quality gates
- Manage AI testing tool budget, vendor evaluations, and AI testing platform contracts
- Champion shift-left AI quality — embedding quality gates at requirements, design, and AI model training stages
- Represent QA leadership in AI governance, ethics boards, and risk committees
- Partner with engineering leadership, product management, and ML/data science leadership to align on quality expectations
- Build responsible AI testing programs in collaboration with legal, compliance, and ethics teams
- Hire, mentor, and develop talent — define career ladders for AI QA specializations
- Contribute to external thought leadership: blogs, conference talks, standards contributions
- Monitor AI industry trends and evolve the team's practices as AI technology advances
