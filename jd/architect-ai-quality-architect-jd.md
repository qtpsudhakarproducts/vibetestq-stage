## Job Description
We are seeking an AI Quality Architect — a visionary senior technical leader who defines the future of quality engineering for AI-first organizations. This is a principal-level role with enterprise-wide scope, responsible for designing quality systems, standards, and architectures that scale across AI product lines, ML platform teams, and regulatory environments. You will be the highest technical authority on quality for AI systems within the organization.

## Responsibilities

### Architecture & Strategy
- Design the enterprise AI quality architecture: evaluation platforms, testing infrastructure, quality data systems, and observability frameworks
- Own the AI Quality Engineering Roadmap — 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year strategic vision aligned with AI product strategy
- Define enterprise-wide AI testing standards and patterns — consumed by every product team building AI-powered features
- Architect scalable LLM evaluation platforms: automated evaluation pipelines, golden dataset management systems, quality SLA monitoring
- Design responsible AI quality architecture: bias testing infrastructure, safety evaluation pipelines, human review workflows

### Governance & Compliance
- Architect AI compliance quality programs for EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and sector-specific AI regulations
- Design AI audit trail systems and quality evidence collection for regulatory submissions and enterprise governance
- Lead AI risk assessment frameworks and quality risk matrices for enterprise AI system portfolios
- Represent quality engineering in executive AI governance bodies and external regulatory forums

### Platform & Infrastructure
- Design internal AI quality platforms: test data management systems, model quality dashboards, AI test environments
- Define AI testing infrastructure standards: GPU test sandboxes, inference server testing environments, agent simulation platforms
- Architect CI/CD quality systems for AI/ML: quality gates, benchmark regression pipelines, automated evaluation workflows
- Define test environment strategies for complex AI systems: multi-model pipelines, RAG systems, multi-agent architectures

### Organization & Culture
- Design the organizational structure and career ladders for AI quality engineering functions
- Establish a Quality Engineering Center of Excellence (CoE) for AI testing knowledge sharing, standards, and best practices
- Define hiring standards, skills frameworks, and competency models for AI quality roles
- Mentor and develop lead-level and senior AI quality engineers through technical guidance and career coaching

### Thought Leadership
- Publish research, white papers, and blog posts on AI quality engineering advancements
- Represent the organization at industry conferences (NeurIPS, ICLR, AI Quality summits, ISTQB conferences)
- Contribute to industry standards bodies (IEEE AI Testing standards, ISTQB AI Testing certification development)
- Build academic and research partnerships to advance AI testing science
