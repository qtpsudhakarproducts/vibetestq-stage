## Job Description
We are seeking a Senior AI Quality Engineer who will take ownership of AI-native quality strategy and drive the organization's quality engineering capabilities into the AI era. You will architect intelligent testing ecosystems, define AI quality standards, mentor teams on AI tool adoption, and ensure product quality for both AI-augmented SDLC workflows and AI-powered product features.

## Responsibilities
- Own and drive the AI quality engineering strategy for the organization — defining standards, toolchain, and adoption roadmap
- Architect AI-native test automation frameworks that leverage GitHub Copilot, LLMs, and self-healing platforms at scale
- Design GenAI-powered test generation pipelines that convert requirements, specs, and user stories into executable test suites
- Lead implementation of intelligent CI/CD quality gates: AI-driven flake detection, intelligent test selection, anomaly-based alerting
- Define AI quality benchmarks and KPIs: test generation velocity, automation coverage uplift, defect escape rate reduction
- Serve as SME for AI-powered product testing: validate LLM integrations, AI API behaviors, and GenAI feature quality
- Partner with product and engineering leadership to embed quality into AI SDLC — from AI-assisted requirements to AI-assisted deployment
- Lead adoption of AI observability platforms for real-time quality insights in production
- Build and maintain shift-left practices for AI: code review quality tools, AI-assisted static analysis, requirement review automation
- Mentor a team of junior and mid-level AI QA engineers on AI toolchain, prompt engineering, and automated test design
- Evaluate and recommend emerging AI testing tools, platforms, and methodologies
- Represent quality engineering in AI governance discussions and AI ethics reviews
- Produce quarterly AI quality reports: coverage, productivity metrics, tool ROI, and quality trend analysis
