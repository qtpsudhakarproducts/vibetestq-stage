## Description
We are seeking an experienced Lead Test Lead to oversee and manage the testing activities for complex software projects. The ideal candidate will have a proven track record in leading testing teams, implementing comprehensive testing strategies, and ensuring high-quality software delivery. You will be responsible for guiding the team through all phases of testing, from planning to execution and reporting, while collaborating closely with development, product, and business stakeholders.

## Responsibilities
- Lead AI-era QA teams, driving adoption of AI tools (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, AI testing platforms) across the engineering organization
- Define and implement AI-first test strategies: AI-generated test suites, self-healing automation, and intelligent CI/CD quality gates
- Develop and implement comprehensive test strategies, plans, and methodologies for large-scale AI-powered projects
- Oversee design, development, and execution of manual and automated tests enhanced by AI assistance
- Own quality validation for AI-powered features: LLM chatbots, AI recommendations, GenAI content generation, and agentic workflows
- Establish and maintain AI quality gates in CI/CD pipelines and AI SDLC workflows
- Lead responsible AI testing programs: validate AI features for safety, bias, fairness, and regulatory compliance
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including AI/ML engineering, product management, and operations
- Conduct performance, security, and API testing for complex AI-integrated applications
- Manage defect tracking including AI-specific defects: hallucination reports, model drift, AI safety incidents
- Ensure compliance with quality standards, AI governance policies, and regulatory requirements (EU AI Act awareness)
- Lead root cause analysis for critical defects including AI behavior regressions and model quality issues
- Define AI testing competency framework and mentor team on AI testing skills
- Build AI testing playbooks, checklists, and standards for consistent quality practices
- Work on specialized testing for banking, healthcare, ServiceNow, and other domains with AI-powered features
