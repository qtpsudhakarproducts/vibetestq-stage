4 Types of QA Professional in the AI Era

AI didn't create one type of QA professional. It created a spectrum. Where you sit on it today determines where you go next.

🧱 Type 1

Hasn't Upgraded Yet

Works exactly as before. Manual testing, manual scripts, no AI in the workflow. Not because of reluctance — simply hasn't started yet.

💬 Type 2

Chat-Only User

Uses ChatGPT or Claude to ask questions, get explanations, maybe draft a test case — but hasn't connected AI to an actual QA workflow yet.

🤝 Type 3

AI-Assisted

Uses AI tools as part of their daily QA work — prompts for test cases, Copilot for automation, agents for exploration. AI is a regular collaborator.

🏗️ Type 4

Workflow Designer

Designs the AI-powered quality system itself. Defines how agents work, how coverage is governed, how the team uses AI — operates at the architecture level.

Exploratory Learning

How this program works — and why

Use first  ·  Notice  ·  Understand  ·  Master

Exploratory Learning

Step 1

Use

Open the tool. Try it. Do something real with it — before anyone explains how it works.

Step 2

Notice

What surprised you? What worked? What didn't? What questions did the experience create?

Step 3

Understand

Now the theory lands — because you've already felt the problem it solves.

Step 4

Master

Use again with understanding. Each iteration deepens both skill and confidence.

Why this works for AI specifically

  • AI is experiential — reading about it means nothing until you've used it
  • Fear of AI dissolves the moment you get a useful output
  • The tool changes faster than any curriculum — instinct travels better than theory

What this means for you in this program

  • Theory never comes first — it always follows a real experience
  • Confusion is not a sign you're behind — it means you're about to understand something
  • Every session produces something real in your hands

You Have Been Doing This Your Whole Career

It's called Exploratory Testing. Same instinct. New territory.
