Risks and the answer
28Chapter: recover without hiding defectsno clicks

29Healing alone vs verified healingno clicks
  • Healing alone: "I found a way to continue." Verified: "I changed the test and proved it works without healing."
  • Pick one risk and make it concrete: the wrong element, like Login with SSO from slide 24.
  • "Self healing without verification creates false confidence." That is the title of this talk.
30What healing must never touch4 clicks
Clicks:
  1. The heal sweeps down: click, fill, read values, popups. All can heal.
  2. It stops at expect and the wait. Blocked.
  3. Assertions: "fixing" one hides a real bug. Waits: a timeout can be the right answer.
  4. "Heal the path to the check. Never touch the check."
31The answer7 clicks
  • Say "Here is the answer to our question" and reveal the six principles one by one.
Clicks: heal actions only; heal by intent; change how, never what; show every heal; verify with healing off; don't force a heal. Then "Recover the step. Never hide the defect."
