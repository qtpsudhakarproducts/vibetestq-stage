Step 1: Inspect (MCP)

AI uses MCP to "See" the DOM and generate resilient locators (Role-based).

Step 2: Generate

AI writes the logic, handling imports, fixtures, and assertions automatically.

Step 3: Self-Heal

If test fails, feed the error back to AI. "Debug why this failed." AI fixes it.

Step 4: Validate

Human Review. Ensure security and business logic are correct.

Why Playwright + GenAI?

Smart Locators

AI prefers getByRole (Accessibility) over brittle XPaths.

Auto-Wait Magic

Playwright waits for elements. AI writes logic. 0% Flakiness.

Visual Defense

AI can generate "Visual Regression" assertions instantly.

Impact: 40% Faster Authoring, 60% Less Maintenance.
